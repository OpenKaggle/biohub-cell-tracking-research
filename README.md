# [2026-09] BioHub Cell Tracking Research Archive

> **Public archive snapshot.** This repository is a reviewable record of the
> research code, tests, methods, and lightweight receipts that were selected
> for publication. It is not a complete competition workspace or a claim of

Public source and reproducibility archive for the [BioHub Cell Tracking During Development](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/biohub-cell-tracking-during-development) research workspace.

## Contribution summary

This snapshot contributes research code, tests, methods, campaign records, and
lightweight provenance receipts. Competition data, generated artifacts,
submissions, and the upstream clone remain excluded and source-linked.

Included: original scripts, tests, methods, campaign records, and lightweight provenance receipts. Excluded: competition data, generated artifacts, virtual environments, submissions, and the upstream `royerlab/kaggle-cell-tracking-competition` clone. The upstream clone is referenced rather than republished.
