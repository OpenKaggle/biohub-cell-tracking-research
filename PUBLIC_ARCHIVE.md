# Public archive scope

This repository records the original research logic, tests, documentation, and lightweight evidence for BioHub Cell Tracking. Competition inputs and generated outputs are excluded from ordinary Git hosting; they are not needed to inspect the methods or provenance trail. The official upstream implementation is referenced by its own repository rather than duplicated here.

Portfolio index: [kaggle-research-portfolio](https://github.com/openkaggle/kaggle-research-portfolio). Related archives: [Nemotron reasoning](https://github.com/openkaggle/nemotron-reasoning-research), [NeuroGolf ONNX](https://github.com/openkaggle/neurogolf-2026-onnx-research), and [Maze Crawler](https://github.com/openkaggle/maze-crawler-research).
